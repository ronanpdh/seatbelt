# 0.0.3: policy at the tool boundary

Released 2026-09-17. Build notes: [docs/BUILD.md](../docs/BUILD.md#003-policy-at-the-tool-boundary).

## What shipped

- A policy engine at the tool boundary, `seatbelt.policy.engine`. Every rule is evaluated on each `Recorder.tool_call` and recorded as a `policy.check`. Any denial raises `PolicyDenied` before the tool body runs.
- Fail closed: a rule that raises counts as a denial, and is recorded as one.
- Ledger hardening:
  - `Event` and `Actor` forbid unknown keys;
  - redaction is anchored to token shapes;
  - files are created mode 0600 and fsynced;
  - appends are thread-safe.
- The Anthropic adapter covers `messages.stream()`, `AsyncAnthropic` and the beta endpoint.
- `reconstruct` verifies before it prints.

## Plan vs what shipped

- The plan had YAML rule files with `allow`, `deny` and `require_approval` effects. What shipped is rules as Python callables, with no approval flow.
- The `Adapter` protocol from 0.0.2 was removed, because nothing consumed it.

## Worth studying

- Why a buggy rule should deny, not allow: `Policy.evaluate` in `src/seatbelt/policy/engine.py`.
- Why an ignored extra key was a security bug. The canonical form is a re-dump of the parsed model, so a key injected into a ledger line survived `verify` (CHANGELOG 0.0.3, Security).
- The race behind "thread-safe appends": concurrent appends previously corrupted the chain with sequence gaps (CHANGELOG 0.0.3, Fixed).
- Why the adapters record no policy checks: they only observe tool calls the framework dispatches.
