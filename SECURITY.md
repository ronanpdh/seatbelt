# Security policy

## Supported versions

Only the latest minor release receives fixes while the project is pre-1.0.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email connect@rpdh.uk with the subject "harness security". Include steps to reproduce and the version or commit.

You will get an acknowledgement within 3 working days and a target fix date within 10. We will credit you in the changelog unless you ask us not to.

Please do not open public issues for security problems, and do not test against systems you do not own or have permission to test.

## Scope

In scope: the harness code, its CLI, the ledger, attestation and evidence pack formats, and the shipped scenarios.

Out of scope: vulnerabilities in target agents or third-party frameworks the harness tests. Report those to their maintainers.

## Design

The maintainer is familiar with secure design principles (least privilege, fail closed, untrusted input at every boundary, no security through obscurity) and with the common classes of vulnerability (the OWASP Top 10, the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications, CWE Top 25). The harness applies them: ledger, sidecar and pack contents are treated as untrusted on read, policy evaluation fails closed, secrets are redacted before anything is hashed or written, files holding keys or records are created `0600` with `O_EXCL`, and archives are checked for path traversal and duplicate entries before extraction.

## Cryptography

SHA-256 for hash chains and content digests, Ed25519 for attestation and pack signatures, both through the [cryptography](https://cryptography.io/) library and Python's `hashlib`. Keys are generated by the library's CSPRNG. No passwords are stored. Releases are signed with Sigstore and carry SLSA provenance.
